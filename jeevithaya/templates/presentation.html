Member 1: Problem Statement Slide 2 of 15

Problem Statement & Motivation

Why traditional blood banking fails emergency medicine

  • Siloed & Paper-Based Registers: Inability to locate rare blood types (e.g. O-, AB-) across regional healthcare networks during emergency surgery.
  • Cold-Chain Expiration Wastage: Units expire unnoticed in cold storage due to lack of automated shelf-life tracking.
  • Transfusion Safety Hazards: Clerical errors during screening can lead to catastrophic transfusion-transmitted infections (TTIs).
  • Communication Latency: Surgical wards experience dangerous delays awaiting phone confirmations for cross-matched blood availability.

Member 2: Data Modeling Slide 5 of 15

Database Design & Entity Relationships

Normalized relational schema (3NF)

  • User: Institutional accounts with role-based access delegation.
  • Donor & Patient: Medical demographics, blood group records, and contact numbers.
  • Donation: Phlebotomy sessions linking donors to collection blood banks.
  • Inventory: Cold-chain blood units with batch numbers, expiry dates, and statuses.
  • Verification: Quality control lab tests (HIV, Hepatitis B/C, Syphilis).
  • BloodRequest: Patient transfusion requisitions categorized by urgency.
  • Disposal: Statutory hazardous waste destruction logs.

Member 3: OOP Principles Slide 7 of 15

OOP: Abstraction & Inheritance

Eliminating redundancy with abstract domain hierarchies

1. Abstraction

• `BaseAuditableEntity`: Abstract model encapsulating `CreatedAt` and `UpdatedAt` timestamps.

• `BaseService`: Abstract base class enforcing standardized data retrieval methods across domain services.

2. Inheritance

• `BasePerson`: Abstract model containing demographic attributes (`Name`, `BloodGroup`, `Gender`, `Address`, `calculate_age`).

• `Donor` and `Patient` inherit directly from `BasePerson`, enforcing schema consistency.

Member 3: OOP Principles Slide 8 of 15

OOP: Encapsulation & Rich Domain Models

Bundling data and validation behavior within entities

  • Password Security Encapsulation: `User.set_password()` and `User.check_password()` encapsulate PBKDF2-SHA256 hashing internally.
  • Cold-Chain Expiration Logic: `Inventory.is_expired()` and `days_until_expiry()` encapsulate shelf-life calculations.
  • Serological Safety Gate: `Verification.is_safe()` encapsulates multi-marker test validation.
  • Requisition State Machine: `BloodRequest.approve()`, `.fulfill()`, and `.reject()` protect lifecycle state transitions.
Member 3: OOP Principles Slide 9 of 15

OOP: Polymorphism & Strategy Pattern

Extensible reporting architecture in `services.py`

Strategy Design Pattern for Reporting

• Interface: `BaseReportStrategy` declares abstract method `generate(days)`.

• Concrete Strategies:

    1. `InventoryReportStrategy`: Calculates blood group distributions and spoilage.

    2. `DonationReportStrategy`: Analyzes donor intake trends.

    3. `RequestReportStrategy`: Analyzes hospital demand and critical emergency ratios.

• Context Executor: `AnalyticsContext` executes any strategy polymorphically at runtime.

Member 4: Core Workflows Slide 10 of 15

Intake & Laboratory Screening Pipeline

Mandatory quality control firewall

  1. Donor Registration: Front-office registers voluntary donor and confirms eligibility.
  2. Phlebotomy Intake: Collected blood units recorded with volume and component type.
  3. Serological Safety Screening: Lab assistant conducts mandatory tests:
    HIV • Hepatitis B • Hepatitis C • Syphilis (VDRL)
  4. Automated Service Trigger:
    • PASSED: Unit unlocked $\rightarrow$ Status set to `Available` in Inventory.
    • FAILED: Unit quarantined $\rightarrow$ Bio-waste `Disposal` record auto-created.
Member 4: Core Workflows Slide 11 of 15

Requisition & Smart Stock Deduction

Rapid emergency ward fulfillment

  • Triage Categorization: Requisitions flagged as `Normal`, `Urgent`, or `Critical`.
  • Administrative Review: Hospital administrator authorizes clinical request.
  • Automated Compatibility Allocation: `RequestFulfillmentService` locates verified, non-expired units of matching blood group.
  • Stock Deduction: Units transition from `Available` $\rightarrow$ `Issued`, automatically updating live counts.

Member 5: Security Slide 13 of 15

Security Architecture & RBAC

Multi-layered clinical data governance

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Strict separation of duties across 4 profiles (`System Admin`, `Hospital Admin`, `Front Office`, `Lab Assistant`).
  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: PBKDF2 with SHA-256 and cryptographic salts—zero plaintext storage.
  • Session Security: Server-backed Django session management with secure session invalidation on logout.
  • Injection Immunity: Fully parameterized Django ORM queries preventing SQL injection attacks.
